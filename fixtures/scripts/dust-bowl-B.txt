NARRATOR (V.O.)

Pare Lorentz's The Plow That Broke the Plains reached theatres in May 1936. The
Resettlement Administration had spent $19,260 on it. Ten million people saw it in 1937.

Its closing inventory was stark: forty million acres ruined, two hundred million more
badly damaged, out of the four hundred million acres that had once looked endless.

The diagnosis was the same as the pictures: grass ripped out, cattle and sheep too
many, plows where plows did not belong, and machines that made the damage fast.
