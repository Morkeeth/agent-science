The craft of agentic engineering is already being written down by the people doing it.

Karpathy on rules files. Willison on explore, plan, code, commit. Huntley on the Ralph
loop — one unit of work, commit, exit. Anthropic on context as the scarce resource.
Ball: an agent is an LLM, a loop, and enough tokens — make the check real.

These are developer truths. Agent Science is where they get verified against the page
that said them — sourced verbatim, or refused with a cause — and remembered for the
next agent who asks.
