Directive 2012/28/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 October 2012 on certain permitted uses of orphan works.

Member States shall bring into force the laws, regulations and administrative provisions necessary to comply with this Directive by 29 October 2014.

Done at Strasbourg, 25 October 2012.
