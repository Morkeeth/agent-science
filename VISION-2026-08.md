# Agent Science — the spine (2026-08-25, Oscar's framing)

*Oscar, verbatim: "Agent science really needs to be the websearch companion, quick registry of the MOST searched things, that's the Big thing. It's suppose to carry agentic science things, developer truths, science — what's the source of truth."*

## Ruling · 2026-08-31 (Oscar)

**Name stays Agent Science.**  
**Noun:** the **truth layer** for what people **believe** and what they **use** — not a citation wall, not clearance-only. Scientific coach with facts. Companion **(B)** is the way; Clearance / regulatory **(A)** is another truth on that layer. Priority **B**.

Believe = claims held as true. Use = practices and tools that show up in real building. The product verifies both against sources (or refuses), remembers them, and surfaces what the field actually runs — a living truth layer, not a bibliography.

**Inputs are wider than research:** practitioner blogs, official product docs, **GitHub stars / push recency** (adoption signal — not a verdict), fleet `popular` queries, and clearance/regulatory truths. See `docs/TRUTH-LAYER-SOURCES.md` · `truth-dictionary/field-signals.json`.

**Personal shelf:** every user gets `~/.agent-science/truth.db` — indexed asks, promoted truths, field fetches, and Magnet skill verdicts (`helped`/`hurt`/`baseline`). Shared signals feed it; it is *their* memory. See `docs/PERSONAL-TRUTH-DB.md`.

## The one line

**Agent Science is the truth layer for what people believe and use.**  
**That is how its websearch works** — not raw results with links. Ask → find what the field believes and uses (blogs, docs, research, adoption signals) → **sourced verbatim or refuse with why** → remember so the next ask is free. Never invent a citation. The registry compounds: most-believed and most-used truths get cheaper for everyone.

## Why this is the "Big thing" and not just A1

The EU AI Act compliance angle (A1 — the Article 53 register) is one **vertical use case** — a nice, sellable one with a legal deadline. But the **horizontal** is bigger: a verified registry of developer / agentic / scientific truths that anyone doing research or running agents reaches for daily. A1 is a customer; the companion is the product.

- **Carries:** agentic-science claims, developer truths ("does React 19 do X", "what does this RFC actually say"), scientific source-of-truth. The domain is "what is actually true, and where does it say so."
- **Daily-use hook:** the websearch companion. Search → sourced answer or an honest refusal. This is the thing a person opens every day, unlike a compliance filing they touch twice a year.
- **The moat is the registry.** A claim proven — or proven *unprovable* — once is free for everyone afterward. The negative space (what CANNOT be sourced) is the half no fact-check marketplace accumulates.

## This is not a pivot — it is already the architecture

What exists today already IS this, under a different name:
- **The corpus** = 312 claims lifted from the fleet's own websearches (`research-corpus/`). This was always "every websearch becomes a verifiable claim" (Oscar's original idea).
- **The compounding log** (`clearance/refusal_log.py`) = 176 rows, keyed by distinctive term, reused across subjects at 0 Parallel calls. **That is the registry.**
- **`verify_corpus`** = the engine that turns a searched claim into SOURCED / UNSOURCED / UNKNOWN against its own cited source.
- **Refuse-not-score** (`clearance/verdict.py`) = the structural refusal that makes it a source of truth, not a confidence meter.

So the reframe is: stop calling it *only* a "clearance desk for factual production" (narrow, compliance-flavored) and call it what Oscar said — **the websearch companion / registry of verified truths**, with clearance / regulatory / E&O as **truths on that shelf** (one paying vertical), not a competing noun.

**Inspiration already collected (2026-08-31):** Agent Grinder's `PRACTICES-CORPUS` — Karpathy, Willison, Huntley, Ball, Osmani, Anthropic, Hashimoto, CodeScene, arXiv control papers — mirrored into `docs/inspiration/` and seeded as claim+URL pairs in `research-inbox/2026-08-31-agentic-practices.md`. See `docs/INSPIRATION-PRACTICES-2026-08-31.md`. Those are the developer truths the companion should face with first.

## The flywheel (product vision, 2026-08-31)

**Ask → verify once → remember forever → optimize from popularity.**

| Step | What | Cost |
|------|------|------|
| **Lookup** | Dev/agent asks via `science_lookup` | free → cheap → live |
| **Truth dictionary** | SOURCED span + URL, or honest refusal | paid once on first live clear |
| **Compound** | Same query / term / alias hits shelf | **$0** |
| **Popular** | `science_popular` ranks what devs actually ask | analytics only |
| **Optimize** | Alias, ingest, route CELEX/patents for top misses | dev time, saves fleet $ |

The product is not "a search API." It is a **self-tuning truth dictionary** — the most-asked things get cheaper for everyone because the registry learns from real usage. Documentary clearance (hackathon wedge) is one customer of the same shelf; daily dev websearch is the volume.

**Moat:** positive truths (sourced) + negative truths (refused with cause) + popularity signal (what to pre-clear next).
