## What changed

- **Memory/context** — case `c5d4051a667f` v12. Test a concise instruction bundle
  against the existing bundle; do not infer lower total cost from lower output
  tokens. The frozen local context trial records this separately from literature.
- **Retrieval** — case `5b54d783ec1f` v11. Hybrid retrieval remains a candidate for
  a matched-cost trial, not a demonstrated accepted-repair improvement. The
  challenge returned another form of an already inspected financial-QA study.
  It did not justify a reversal. Independent review made the unanswered markdown
  note alternative explicit, including creation, update and stale-note costs.
  [Repository retrieval study](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.24882v1),
  [SWE-Explore](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.07297v1).
- **Coding** — case `988cbb2094ed` v6. The challenge changed the protocol to check
  test-oracle validity and false rejection of valid alternative patches. It did
  not establish a causal benefit of feedback on unseen repository tasks.
  [Self-debugging study](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.881/),
  [SWE-ABS](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.00520v1).
- **UX** — case `7be7dab537cd` v9. Selective escalation is now an explicit policy
  hypothesis. A benchmark oracle and forced information injection do not measure
  real human interruption cost. This was a source-method interpretation correction;
  the newly found survey was not empirical counterevidence.
  [HiL-Bench](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.09408),
  [Clarification timing](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.07937v1).
- **Coordination** — case `6f9c8e80b194` v8. The proposed comparison now includes
  blinded maintainer acceptance and review/repair effort. Equal thinking tokens or
  maximum iterations do not establish equal total resources. The METR source
  refines measurement; it does not estimate the effect of delegation.
  [Single/multi-agent study](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.02460v1).
- **Evaluation** — case `d6ec0a732fd4` v7. Use blinded acceptance alongside tests
  and adjudicate disagreements. Initial methods reading exposed scope limits in
  executable review and merge-rate metrics; the challenge added maintainer review.
  Independent review added failed-test imputation, golden-baseline uncertainty and
  review-protocol caveats. No prospective universal predictor is established.
  [c-CRAB](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.23448v3),
  [PR acceptance](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.08915v2),
  [METR maintainer review](https://metr.org/notes/2026-03-10-many-swe-bench-passing-prs-would-not-be-merged-into-main/).

These practical consequences are authored inferences. A common source used in
several cases remains one study. Mirrors and different formats are not replications.
For every topic, an independently accepted outcome under the intended task and
resource conditions could change the recommendation; the saved cases state the
specific experiment and unresolved gaps.
