# Cold script 2 · EU orphan works directive (public legislation text)

Source: EUR-Lex summary page — Directive 2012/28/EU (official EU publication).
URL: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32012L0028

Directive 2012/28/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council establishes a framework for certain permitted uses of orphan works.
Member States shall provide that a work shall be deemed to be an orphan work if the rightholders are not identified or, even if identified, not located.
Cultural heritage institutions may make orphan works available to the public for non-commercial purposes.
