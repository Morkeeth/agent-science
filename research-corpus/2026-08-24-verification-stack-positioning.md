### Composition honesty (true parts, false whole) — the gap
- [academic] FaithCoT-Bench (ICLR 2026): instance-level unfaithfulness of CoT trajectories; 11 detection methods benchmarked; finds explanations become MORE deceptively plausible as models improve — benchmark, not a product — https://openreview.net/pdf?id=lN3yKqqzF1
- [academic] MASK benchmark: disentangles honesty from accuracy — a model can be accurate on every fact and still lie; motivates monitoring propensity to deceive — academic only — https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.03750
- [academic] Agent plans/tool-use rationales shown to be post-hoc justifications, not causal accounts of the work ("Verify Before You Commit", 2026) — https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.08401
- [academic] Automated fact-checking field (ClaimBuster → 2026 survey) verifies claims against EXTERNAL evidence; no tool audits the internal composition of a self-report (whether two true numbers assert a false relation) — https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2949719124000141
- [claim/synthesis] NO commercial product found in 11 searches that checks an agent report for adjacent-false composition (every number true, the pair a lie, e.g. a headline % over rows that sum to something else). Closest are hallucination detectors (external truth) and CoT-faithfulness benchmarks (academic). This is the white space. — (synthesis of the sources above; falsifiable by Agent Science re-search)

### Authorship (human vs agent turns) — partially occupied
- [primary] Grammarly Authorship + Superhuman (Mar 10, 2026): agent-specific attribution — which parts of a document were human-created, AI-generated, AI-edited — https://www.grammarly.com/blog/company/superhuman-authorship-docs/
- [academic] AgenTag (2026): attributes AI coding agents (Codex, Copilot, Devin, Cursor, Claude Code) from behavioral fingerprints, 97.2% F1 multi-class — https://arxiv.org/html/2608.00966v1
- [academic] PAN 2026 introduced "Reasoning Trajectory Detection" as a formal shared task: attribute reasoning trajectories to LLM vs human authors — https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-21321-1_32
- [claim/synthesis] Authorship attribution alone is NOT white space (Grammarly ships it; PAN formalized it). White space = authorship tied to OUTCOME ("whose prompt produced lasting work" — a practice corpus, not a detector) and COMBINED with composition honesty as one pre-production gate. — (synthesis)
